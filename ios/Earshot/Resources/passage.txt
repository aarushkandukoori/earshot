On Saturday morning the market opens early, and the first thing you notice is the noise. A man is stacking crates of apples beside a woman who sells fresh bread, and a small dog waits patiently under the table for anything that falls. Somebody laughs near the coffee stand while a bicycle bell rings twice. I usually walk the whole length of the street before I buy anything, because the best peaches are never at the front. Later, when the sun is higher and the crowd is thicker, I find a quiet bench, open a paper bag of warm rolls, and listen to the city wake up around me.
